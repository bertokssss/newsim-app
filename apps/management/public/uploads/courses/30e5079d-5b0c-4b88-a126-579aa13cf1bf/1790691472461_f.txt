PIECES	Problems	Opportunities
Performance	Manual batch scheduling can result in scheduling conflicts and delays in organizing training sessions.	Automate batch scheduling based on capacity, training duration, and instructor availability.
Information	Training, attendance, progress, and certification information require manual coordination and may not be readily available in one place.	Provide centralized and updated trainee and training records.
Economy	Manual coordination and administrative processing require additional time and staff effort.	Reduce repetitive administrative work through digital workflows.
Control/Security	Access to training and certification information needs to be controlled according to user responsibilities.	Implement role-based user access and controlled modification of records.
Efficiency	Enrollment, prerequisite checking, attendance monitoring, progress tracking, and certification involve multiple manual steps.	Automate appropriate processes and provide real-time monitoring and status updates.
Service	Trainees and staff rely on manual coordination for schedules, progress, enrollment, and certification status.	Provide a centralized platform where authorized users can access relevant information and updates.
